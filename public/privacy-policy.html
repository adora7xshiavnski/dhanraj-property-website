1. Introduction

Dhanraj Property ("we", "us" or "our") is a real estate agency with its office at 206, Rajendra Nagar, Dhanwantri Nagar, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452012, India. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website, fill in one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, call us, message us on WhatsApp, email us or otherwise use our real estate services, and how we use, share and protect that data.

This policy is framed in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them. By using our website or services, or by submitting a lead form, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is needed to help you with your property requirement:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address, provided through our website enquiry form or our Facebook and Instagram lead forms.
  • Location: your city or area, and your preferred locations for a property.
  • Property requirements: whether you want to buy, sell, rent or invest, your budget, the property type and any other details you choose to share.
  • Property details (for owners, sellers and landlords): property address, ownership details, photographs, price expectations and related documents you choose to share.
  • Transaction documents: where you proceed with a deal, copies of identity and address proof (such as Aadhaar or PAN), sale or rental agreements and other documents required for verification, agreement or registration.
  • Communication records: messages, emails and notes of calls you have with us.
  • Technical information: basic information such as browser type, device type, IP address and pages visited, collected automatically by our website host and by the Meta Pixel described in section 7.

Our website does not process online payments and does not ask for bank, card or UPI details. We will never ask for your OTP or passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your property enquiry and contact you about it by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To find, shortlist and share suitable properties, and to arrange site visits.
  • To market your property to genuine prospective buyers or tenants, if you list it with us.
  • To verify property and party details, and to prepare agreements and documentation.
  • To coordinate with lawyers, banks or loan providers and registration authorities, when you ask us to.
  • To send you updates on relevant properties, where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To measure and improve our advertising on Facebook and Instagram.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect against fraud or misuse.

4. Consent

We process your personal data based on the consent you give when you submit an enquiry or lead form, contact us, or share documents with us, and for legitimate uses permitted by law. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in section 13. Withdrawing consent will not affect processing already carried out, but we may be unable to continue providing services that depend on that data.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only when necessary:

  • With property owners, builders and developers: only for a property transaction you have asked us to help with, for example sharing a buyer's or tenant's contact details with the owner or builder of a property they are interested in (and vice versa).
  • With professionals you ask us to involve: such as advocates, document writers, banks or housing finance companies, and valuers.
  • With service providers: who help us run our website, email and communications, under appropriate confidentiality.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

6. Third-Party Services

We use a few third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Facebook and Instagram lead forms (Meta Platforms): when you submit one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta collects the details you enter and makes them available to us.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you click a WhatsApp button or send an enquiry via WhatsApp, your message is sent through WhatsApp.
  • EmailJS and Google (Gmail): when you send the enquiry form on our website, your details are processed by EmailJS, an email delivery service, and delivered to our inbox through Google's Gmail service.
  • Vercel: our website is hosted by Vercel, which may log technical information such as your IP address.
  • Google Maps and Google Fonts: the map on our contact section and the fonts on our website are loaded from Google, which may collect usage data such as your IP address.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties or of other websites linked from our site.

7. Cookies and Meta Pixel

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms that sets cookies and records page visits and actions such as sending an enquiry, tapping our call, WhatsApp or email links, or using the property search. It sends this information, along with technical details such as your IP address, browser and device, to Meta. We do not send your name, phone number or message to Meta through the Pixel. We use it to measure and improve our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, including showing our ads to people who have visited our website.

Embedded third-party services (such as Google Maps) may also set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, and manage how Meta uses this data in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences. The website will continue to work.

8. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, for example while your enquiry or transaction is active, and afterwards for as long as required by law (such as for tax or legal records). When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable physical, technical and administrative measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse, including limiting access to people who need it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a personal data breach, we will act as required by applicable law.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Correction: ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: ask us to erase your data, unless we must retain it by law.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Opt out of property updates and marketing messages.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

11. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age without verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. If you believe a child has shared data with us, please contact us and we will delete it.
